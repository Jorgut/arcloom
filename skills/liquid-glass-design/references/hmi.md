# HMI Constraints

For automotive, industrial, cockpit, or spatial interfaces:

- Keep primary driving, alarm, emergency, and irreversible controls opaque unless the applicable design system explicitly validates translucency.
- Prioritize glanceability and state recognition over material realism.
- Test sunlight, dark adaptation, display dimming, glare, vibration, gloves, and hardware input states where relevant.
- Avoid pointer-following effects on controls operated without a pointer.
- Ensure warnings and disabled states remain unambiguous when transparency is reduced.
- Treat the web component as a prototype unless it has been validated on target hardware and against applicable standards.
